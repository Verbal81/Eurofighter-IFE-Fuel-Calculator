AAR EVENT DURATION: verschiebt alle nachfolgenden ETA-Zeiten und erzeugt jetzt zusätzlich den Eigenverbrauch während des AAR. Required Onload = Target AAR-OUT FOB − FOB IN + AAR-duration burn. Der AAR-Burn wird aus AAR-Höhe, KIAS, Konfiguration und dem aktiven Fuel-Flow-Modell berechnet.

SECURITY & PRIVACY

LOCAL FIRST

No app account, no advertising and no analytics/tracking SDKs.

Mission data and settings are stored locally on this device.

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, payment or USB access.

SimBrief is contacted only when you press Import. Pilot ID/Alias is used; no SimBrief password is requested or stored.

PRIVACY POLICY
No security guarantee is claimed; the build is designed to minimize permissions and external dependencies.
